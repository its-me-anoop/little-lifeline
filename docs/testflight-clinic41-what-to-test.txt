Little Lifeline 4.1 — A longer climb and rooms that change as they grow

Your existing clinic, coins and upgrades carry over. Please update over 4.0 rather than reinstalling, so the save upgrade is tested too.

Please try:
• Rooms now grow to 20 sizes (40 in the doctors clinic). Renovate a room and watch its floor, walls and furniture restyle at every size.
• Equipment and facilities go to level 10 (20 in the doctors clinic), and staff training to level 25 (50). Each size opens a few more levels; every level adds something you can see.
• Decor is now optional and bought with gems. Open a room, choose the Decor tab and add a piece. It adds a little to every visit fee but is never needed to progress.
• Train a member of staff a few times and look for the dots, stars and shoulder boards on their uniform.
• Try the refreshed screens: the balance capsule, clinic chip, next-step card, room panel tabs, and the gems, clinics and settings sheets.
• After a longer break, check the welcome-back card and the Finish buttons for any building still under way.

Opening the doctors clinic now needs every starter room at size 20, so expect this to take a good while.

Please report balances or prices that look wrong, anything that overlaps or vanishes, rooms that do not change when renovated, and any screen that is hard to read or tap.
