Little Lifeline 4.2 — Twenty pieces of equipment in every room

Your existing clinic, coins and upgrades carry over. Please update over 4.1 rather than reinstalling so the save upgrade is tested too. Nothing you own gets slower.

Please try:
• Open any room and choose Equipment. Every room now has twenty pieces, and each renovation unlocks one more. Every piece has ten versions, from basic to advanced. Tap a piece to see it in the picture at the top, then upgrade it and watch the new version appear in the room.
• Each upgrade takes a tenth off the time the room still has to lose, so the first upgrades matter most. The room shows its check-in, treatment, call, consultation or dispensing time.
• The Facilities tab is gone. Queue places, waiting seats and the visit fee now grow with each room's equipment.
• In the doctors clinic, look for coins over the pharmacy counters and the taxi stand. Patients pay for their prescriptions and for each taxi ride; tap the coins to collect them.
• New clinics start with 250 coins and 5 gems.
• Visitors now wait in line inside the reception, not out on the pavement.

Please report anything that overlaps, floats or hides another piece; prices or times that look wrong; and coins you cannot collect.
