Little Lifeline 4.0 is a miniature 3D clinic management game. No account, password, external hardware or payment is needed, and the simulation works offline. The game is fictional and provides no medical advice.

What's new for review: in-app purchases. This version adds four consumable gem packs (com.flutterly.gravitile.gems.small, .medium, .large, .xl) and one non-consumable, the Second Builder (com.flutterly.gravitile.builder). All five are attached to this version.

How to find them:
1. Launch and wait for the first patient to pay at reception, then tap the cash marker to collect it.
2. Tap the gem counter next to the coin wallet at the top. This opens Gems & goals with three tabs: Today (daily goals and login streak), Goals (milestones) and Shop.
3. The Shop tab lists the four gem packs with App Store prices, then items bought with gems: coin top-ups, double collections for 1 or 4 hours, longer offline earnings, and the Second Builder, which can be bought with money or unlocked with 400 gems earned in play.
4. To see gems spent in play: tap a room, choose Renovate, then Finish beside the construction timer.

Gems are awarded free for milestones, daily goals and the login streak, so every item can be reached without paying. Every room, upgrade and clinic can also be earned with game coins alone. There are no random rewards, loot boxes, advertisements or subscriptions.

Purchases: a gem transaction is finished only after its gems are saved on the device, so an interrupted purchase is delivered on the next launch. Refunded purchases remove the unspent gems. Restore Purchases re-applies the Second Builder. Gems are consumable and stored on the device; the shop says so.

Existing players keep their saved clinic. Milestones they reached before updating can be collected as gems.
