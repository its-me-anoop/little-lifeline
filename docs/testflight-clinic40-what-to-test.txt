Little Lifeline 4.0 — Goals, gems and a more lifelike neighbourhood

Your existing clinic, coins and upgrades carry over. Please update over 3.3 rather than reinstalling, so the save upgrade is tested too.

Please try:
• Tap the gem counter next to your coins. Collect the goals you have already reached, then check the next goals and the gem shop.
• Start a room renovation and use Finish to complete it with gems. It is free in the last minute.
• Renovations now take longer at higher tiers (up to eight hours) and later room upgrades cost more. Income is unchanged.
• Park and leave: cars now pay at the exit barrier. Collect the coins from the pay machine marker or the car park panel.
• Watch patients leave on foot: they should walk away down the street or across the crossing, not vanish at the door.
• Look around the new people, cars, houses, car park and entrance on both clinics.

The gem shop's App Store packs are not live yet in this build, so the shop may say it is loading. Goals and Finish work without purchases.

Please report incorrect balances, gems not appearing after a goal, anything that vanishes or overlaps, or anything that looks wrong in the new scenery.
